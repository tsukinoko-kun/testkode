## Ode to ESC

In a terminal dark and deep,
where bytes and pixels meet,
a silent stream begins to speak
in notes of pure escape.

ESC, [code, m — the hidden key
that paints the plain screen bright,
turning blank walls to a fantasy
of green and red and white.

`\x1b[31m` the crimson cry,
`\x1b[32m` the garden green,
`\x1b[33m` the amber sky,
`\x1b[34m` the ocean's sheen.

Bold and bright, on and off,
they blink, they blink, and move,
while the cursor dances to scoff
at every squalid groove.

So when the screen goes dark and cold,
and colors start to fade,
remember the sequence, bold and old,
that made your pixels cascade.
